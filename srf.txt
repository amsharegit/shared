.DISCUSSION...
A north-northwest swell will continue a slow decline through 
Saturday, and surf along exposed north and west facing shores should 
drop below High Surf Advisory thresholds this afternoon or evening. A 
reinforcing medium period energy out of the north-northwest (330 to 
340 degree) should arrive late Saturday into Sunday. Surf could 
approach advisory thresholds during its peak early Sunday morning. 
Another large northwest to north-northwest swell is Wednesday or 
Thursday of next week.

A small south-southwest swell should produce south shore surf 
slightly above seasonal average through Saturday. A tiny pulse from 
the south-southeast may maintain surf near seasonal average Sunday and
Monday, followed by background level surf through the remainder of the
week.

Surf along east-facing shores will stay elevated and choppy through 
much of the week due to the strong trades. Select northeast exposures 
should see long-period energy from the large north-northwest swell 
into tonight.
