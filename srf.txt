Surf Zone Forecast for Hawaii
National Weather Service Honolulu HI
852 AM HST Fri Nov 20 2026

.DISCUSSION...
Small surf will prevail along north- and west-facing shores for the
next few days followed by much larger swells next week. A tiny north
swell will be overlapped by a small north-northwest that will peak on
Sunday and decline on Monday. A large northwest swell will build on
Tuesday and will push surf along north- and west-facing shores well
above the High Surf Advisory threshold into Wednesday. An extra large
northwest swell is possible on Thanksgiving Day, though a front 
likely moving down the island chain at the same time could bring 
fresh northerly winds.

Strong trade winds will maintain rough surf just below the High Surf
Advisory level along east facing shores through Saturday. As trades
decline, east shore surf will fall below seasonal average Sunday and
will be very small through Wednesday. A small, long-period swell will
bring a bump to south shore surf through Saturday, with mainly
background level surf expected late Sunday through early next week.
